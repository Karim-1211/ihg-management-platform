# Customer Guide

## 1. Quick Start

Most days, you'll do one of these things:
- Check your bookings, orders, or bills → go to **My Account**
- Book a room → go to **Hotel** menu → browse hotels
- Order food → go to **Restaurant** or **Delivery** menu

## 2. Logging In

1. Go to the login page.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Log In**.

Don't have an account? Click **Register** on the login page to create one.

## 3. Navigation

At the top of every page you'll see:
- **Hotel** — browse hotels, see your bookings, see your hotel invoices
- **Restaurant** — browse restaurants, see your table reservations
- **Delivery** — order food for delivery
- **My Activity** — takes you to **My Account**, your central hub for everything
- Your name (top right) — opens **My Account** or **Sign out**
- The bell icon — shows your recent notifications

**My Account** has six tabs: Bookings, Orders, Reservations, Invoices, Service Requests, Notifications.

## 4. Daily Tasks

### Booking a Hotel Room
1. Click **Hotel** → **Browse Hotels**.
2. Choose a hotel and click **View Rooms**.
3. Pick your check-in and check-out dates and click **Search Rooms**.
4. Click **Book Now** on the room you want.

There is no "new booking" button inside My Account — booking always starts by browsing hotels.

### Viewing or Cancelling a Booking
1. Go to **My Account → Bookings**.
2. Find your booking in the list.
3. To cancel: click **Cancel Booking**, then confirm in the pop-up.

Cancellation is only available while your booking is **CONFIRMED** or **CHECKED_IN**. Cancellation is subject to the hotel's cancellation policy — ask the assistant or reception if you're unsure how much notice is required.

### Requesting Room Service (housekeeping, towels, food, etc.)
This is only available once you're checked in.
1. Go to **My Account → Bookings**.
2. Find your active stay and click **Request Service**.
3. Choose the type of request (Room Cleaning, Extra Towels, Water, Food, Amenities, Other) and add a note if you like.
4. Click **Request**.

You can see the status of your requests in **My Account → Service Requests**.

### Ordering Food (Dine-In)
1. Click **Restaurant**, choose a restaurant.
2. Add items to **Your Order** using the + button.
3. Select your table from the dropdown.
4. Click **Place Order**.

### Ordering Delivery
1. Click **Delivery**, choose a restaurant.
2. Add items to your order.
3. Enter your delivery address.
4. Click **Place Order**.

You can track your delivery's progress in **My Account → Orders** — it will show the delivery status (assigned, picked up, out for delivery, delivered) and the driver's name once assigned.

### Reserving a Table
1. Click **Restaurant**, choose a restaurant, click **Reserve a Table**.
2. Choose a table, date, time, and party size.
3. Click **Reserve Table**.

To cancel a reservation, go to **My Account → Reservations** and click **Cancel**.

### Paying a Bill
1. Go to **My Account → Invoices**.
2. You'll see both hotel bills and restaurant bills together.
3. Click **Pay Now** on any unpaid invoice.

This platform currently processes payments in demo mode — no real charge occurs.

### Using the Guest Assistant
Click the chat icon in the bottom-right corner of any page. You can ask things like:
- "What are my current bookings?"
- "What's on the menu at [restaurant]?"
- "Can I still cancel my booking?"

The assistant can answer questions and explain policies, but **cannot** book, cancel, or pay anything for you — it will always point you to the right screen to do it yourself.

## 5. Status Meanings

**Bookings:** Confirmed (upcoming, not yet checked in) · Checked In (you're currently staying) · Checked Out (stay complete) · Cancelled

**Orders:** New (just placed) · Confirmed (kitchen has it) · Preparing · Ready · Served/Delivered · Cancelled

**Reservations:** Confirmed · Seated · Completed · Cancelled · No Show

**Invoices:** Unpaid · Partially Paid · Paid

## 6. Common Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Cancel Booking" button isn't showing | It only appears while the booking is Confirmed or Checked In — already-completed or cancelled bookings can't be cancelled again |
| I can't find a "new booking" option in My Account | Booking always starts from the **Hotel** menu, not from My Account |
| My delivery status isn't updating | Delivery status updates as the restaurant/driver progress the order — check back in a few minutes, or ask the Guest Assistant |
| I don't see my invoice yet | Hotel invoices are generated at checkout; restaurant/delivery invoices are generated once your order is marked Served/Delivered |

## 7. Important Notes

- Your notification bell shows updates on bookings, orders, and payments — click a notification to jump straight to the relevant My Account tab.
- Cancelling one browser tab's chat with the Guest Assistant doesn't affect your bookings — it only clears the chat conversation.
- Signing out clears your chat conversation with the assistant, for your privacy on shared devices.

## 8. Your Permissions

As a Customer, you can view and manage only your own bookings, orders, reservations, invoices, and service requests. You cannot see other guests' information, staff screens, or business management tools.
