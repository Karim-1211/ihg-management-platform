# Waiter Guide

## 1. Quick Start

Your main screens are **Tables**, **Orders**, **Reservations**, **Bills**, and **Deliveries**. You manage the dining room: seating, taking orders through to serving, and assigning delivery drivers.

## 2. Logging In

1. Go to the login page.
2. Enter the email and password given to you by your manager.
3. Click **Log In**.

## 3. Navigation

Your sidebar shows:
- **Tables** — table status (available, occupied, cleaning)
- **Orders** — dine-in and delivery order queue
- **Reservations** — table bookings
- **Bills** — restaurant invoices
- **Deliveries** — assign drivers to delivery orders
- **Inventory** — view current stock levels
- **Notification Settings** — control which notifications you receive

## 4. Daily Tasks

### Managing Table Status
1. Go to **Tables**.
2. You can see and update each table's status (Available, Occupied, Cleaning).

### Progressing an Order
1. Go to **Orders**.
2. Move dine-in orders through their stages: Confirm the order (sends it to the kitchen), and once the kitchen marks it Ready, mark it **Served**.

### Assigning a Delivery Driver
1. Go to **Deliveries**.
2. Find a delivery order (kitchen must have marked it Ready first).
3. Assign an available driver.

The delivery then moves through the driver's own workflow (Picked Up → Out for Delivery → Delivered), and the order is automatically marked Served once delivered.

### Handling Reservations
1. Go to **Reservations** to see upcoming table bookings.
2. When guests arrive, you can update the reservation status (e.g., to Seated).

### Handling Bills
1. Go to **Bills** to see restaurant invoices — these are generated automatically once an order is marked Served.

## 5. Status Meanings

**Orders:** New · Confirmed · Preparing · Ready · Served · Cancelled

**Tables:** Available · Occupied · Cleaning

**Reservations:** Confirmed · Seated · Completed · Cancelled · No Show

**Deliveries:** Pending · Assigned · Picked Up · Out for Delivery · Delivered

## 6. Common Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I can't mark a delivery order as Served | Delivery orders are marked Served automatically once the driver completes delivery — you don't do this manually |
| No drivers available to assign | Check with your Manager about driver scheduling |
| A table won't update | Make sure there's no active order still tied to that table |

## 7. Important Notes

- Delivery orders follow a different completion path than dine-in orders — the driver's "Delivered" action is what completes them, not you marking them Served directly.
- Bills generate automatically; you don't create them manually.

## 8. Your Permissions

As a Waiter, you can manage tables, orders, reservations, restaurant bills, and delivery assignment, and view inventory stock levels. You cannot access kitchen-only screens, hotel bookings, or business reports.
