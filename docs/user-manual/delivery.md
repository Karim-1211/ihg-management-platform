# Delivery Staff Guide

## 1. Quick Start

Your main screen is **My Deliveries** — it shows every delivery currently assigned to you. Move each one through: Picked Up → Out for Delivery → Delivered.

## 2. Logging In

1. Go to the login page.
2. Enter the email and password given to you by your manager.
3. Click **Log In**.

## 3. Navigation

Your sidebar shows:
- **My Deliveries** — your assigned deliveries
- **Notification Settings** — control which notifications you receive

## 4. Daily Tasks

### Completing a Delivery
1. Go to **My Deliveries**.
2. A waiter assigns deliveries to you — once assigned, you'll see it in your list with status **Assigned**.
3. When you collect the order, mark it **Picked Up**.
4. When you leave for the customer's address, mark it **Out for Delivery**.
5. When you've handed it over, mark it **Delivered**.

Marking a delivery as Delivered automatically marks the underlying order as Served and generates the customer's invoice — you don't need to do anything else.

## 5. Status Meanings

**Deliveries:** Pending (created, not yet assigned) · Assigned (assigned to you) · Picked Up · Out for Delivery · Delivered · Cancelled

## 6. Common Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I can't update a delivery that isn't assigned to me | Only the driver a delivery is assigned to can update its status — check with the waiter if you think you should have it |
| A delivery I completed still shows in my list | Refresh the page — completed (Delivered) deliveries should disappear from your active list |

## 7. Important Notes

- You'll receive a notification when a new delivery is assigned to you.
- The customer can see your name and the delivery status in their own account once you're assigned.

## 8. Your Permissions

As Delivery staff, you can view and update only the deliveries assigned to you. You cannot see other drivers' deliveries, kitchen orders, or billing information.
