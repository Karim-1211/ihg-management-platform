# Kitchen Staff Guide

## 1. Quick Start

Your main screen is **Kitchen** — it shows every order currently needing preparation. Your job is to move orders through: Confirmed → Preparing → Ready.

## 2. Logging In

1. Go to the login page.
2. Enter the email and password given to you by your manager.
3. Click **Log In**.

## 3. Navigation

Your sidebar shows:
- **Kitchen** — your main working screen
- **Inventory** — view current stock levels
- **Notification Settings** — control which notifications you receive

## 4. Daily Tasks

### Preparing Orders
1. Go to **Kitchen**.
2. You'll see a card for each order, showing the order number, table (for dine-in), and items.
3. Click **Start Preparing** to move an order from Confirmed to Preparing.
4. Once the food is ready, mark the order **Ready**.

The order will then leave your list — a waiter (or, for delivery, the driver) handles it from there.

### Managing Ingredient Stock
1. Go to **Inventory**.
2. Click **Manage** on any item to see its current stock and history.
3. To record a change, choose a type — **Purchase** or **Return** (adds stock), or **Waste**, **Manual Adjustment**, or **Service Usage** (removes stock) — for example, recording waste when an ingredient spoils or is damaged.
4. Enter the quantity and an optional note, then click **Record**.

Stock for recipe-linked menu items is deducted **automatically** when an order is confirmed — you only need to record transactions manually for things like waste, spoilage, or deliveries.

## 5. Status Meanings

**Orders:** New (just placed, not yet in your queue) · Confirmed (ready for you to start) · Preparing (you're working on it) · Ready (done, waiting for pickup/service) · Served/Delivered · Cancelled

## 6. Common Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| An order won't let me confirm/start it | The order may not have enough stock for a recipe-linked item — check Inventory, or ask your manager to restock |
| I see an order that's already someone else's table | Orders are shared across the kitchen team; anyone can work on any order in the queue |
| Delivery order disappeared after I marked it Ready | Delivery orders are automatically marked Served once the driver completes the delivery — this is expected |

## 7. Important Notes

- You cannot manually change an order back to an earlier stage — if you make a mistake, tell your manager.
- The kitchen queue only shows orders that are Confirmed or Preparing — served/cancelled orders won't clutter your view.

## 8. Your Permissions

As Kitchen staff, you can view and update the status of orders and record inventory transactions (purchases, waste, adjustments) for your restaurant. You cannot create new inventory items, access billing, customer accounts, business reports, or other restaurants' orders.
