# Manager Guide

## 1. Quick Start

You have access to everything hotel and restaurant staff can see, plus management tools: **Dashboard** (live snapshot), **Reports** (business performance), **Business Rules** (operating policies), and **Audit Log** (change history). Start your day with the Dashboard to see what needs attention.

## 2. Logging In

1. Go to the login page.
2. Enter your manager email and password.
3. Click **Log In**.

## 3. Navigation

Your sidebar is organized into groups:
- **Management**: Dashboard, Reports, Business Rules, Audit Log, Inventory, Recipes, Notification Settings
- **Hotel**: Bookings, Housekeeping, Service Requests, Invoices
- **Restaurant**: Tables, Orders, Kitchen, Reservations, Bills
- **Delivery**: Deliveries

You'll only see and manage data for **your own hotel** — if you manage one property, your Dashboard, Reports, and Bookings will only ever show that property's data, even though the screens look the same as an Admin's.

## 4. Daily Tasks

### Checking the Dashboard
1. Go to **Dashboard**.
2. See at a glance: today's occupancy, orders, active deliveries, low stock alerts, today's revenue, and anything needing attention (e.g., overdue service requests).

### Reviewing Business Performance
1. Go to **Reports**.
2. Choose a tab: Executive Overview, Hotel, Restaurant, Delivery, Inventory, or Operational.
3. Choose a time period (Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, This Month, Previous Month, or a custom range).

Each figure shows the current value and how it compares to the previous equivalent period.

### Managing Business Rules
1. Go to **Business Rules**.
2. You'll see policies like Tax Rate, Service Request Escalation, Kitchen Bottleneck Threshold, and Reservation Duration.
3. Click a policy to view its current value and history, or to change it.
4. Any change you make is recorded — you'll see who changed it and when in that policy's history.

As a Manager, you can create and edit rules scoped to **your own hotel**, but not global (platform-wide) rules — those are Admin-only.

### Reviewing the Audit Log
1. Go to **Audit Log** to see a record of significant actions (bookings, payments, business rule changes, etc.) across the system.

### All Hotel, Restaurant, and Delivery Tasks
You have the same day-to-day capabilities as Receptionists, Housekeeping, Waiters, Kitchen staff, and the Deliveries screen — see those manuals for step-by-step instructions on bookings, orders, reservations, and deliveries.

### Using the Staff Assistant
Click the chat icon in the bottom-right corner. As a Manager, you can ask for things like:
- "Give me today's business summary."
- "Is anything unusual going on today?"
- "What's the occupancy right now?"

The assistant pulls live figures from the same Dashboard/Reports data — it never invents numbers, and it cannot change any settings or perform actions for you.

## 5. Status Meanings

See the Receptionist, Housekeeping, Waiter, and Kitchen guides for booking/order/task status meanings — they apply identically for you.

**Business Rule status:** Active · Inactive

## 6. Common Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I can't create a Global business rule | Only Admin (Super Admin) can create Global (platform-wide) rules — you can create rules scoped to your own hotel |
| My numbers look different from another manager's | Each Manager only sees their own hotel's data — this is expected |
| A rule change was rejected | The system won't allow two active rules with the same key, scope, and priority that overlap in time — check the existing rule's dates first |

## 7. Important Notes

- Changing a business rule (like the tax rate) takes effect immediately for new transactions — it does not change past invoices.
- The Anomaly summary (via the assistant or your own review of Reports) flags changes exceeding 50% versus the prior period as worth investigating — it's a starting point for review, not a definitive problem report.

## 8. Your Permissions

As a Manager, you can access all Hotel, Restaurant, and Delivery operational screens, the Dashboard, Reports, hotel-scoped Business Rules, the Audit Log, Inventory, and Recipes — all scoped to your own hotel. You cannot manage Global business rules, staff accounts, or the platform-wide email log — those are Admin-only.
