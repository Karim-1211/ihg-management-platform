# Receptionist Guide

## 1. Quick Start

Your main screens are **Bookings** (check guests in/out), **Service Requests** (handle guest requests), and **Invoices** (view hotel bills). Most days you'll check in arriving guests, check out departing guests, and handle any service requests that come in.

## 2. Logging In

1. Go to the login page.
2. Enter the email and password given to you by your manager.
3. Click **Log In**.

## 3. Navigation

Your sidebar shows:
- **Bookings** — all hotel bookings, check-in/check-out
- **Service Requests** — guest requests (cleaning, towels, food, amenities)
- **Invoices** — hotel invoices
- **Inventory** — view current stock levels
- **Notification Settings** — control which notifications you receive

## 4. Daily Tasks

### Checking In a Guest
1. Go to **Bookings**.
2. Find the guest's booking (it will show status **Confirmed**).
3. Click **Check In**, then confirm in the pop-up.

The booking status changes to **Checked In**, and the guest can now request room service.

### Checking Out a Guest
1. Go to **Bookings**.
2. Find the guest's booking (status **Checked In**).
3. Click **Check Out**, then confirm in the pop-up.

This automatically generates the guest's final invoice (room charge plus any completed service requests) and sends the room to Housekeeping for cleaning.

### Cancelling a Booking
1. Go to **Bookings**.
2. Find the booking (must be Confirmed or Checked In).
3. Click **Cancel**, then confirm in the pop-up.

### Finding a Specific Booking
Use the **search box** at the top of the Bookings page to search by booking reference — useful once the list gets long.

### Handling a Service Request
1. Go to **Service Requests**.
2. You'll see all pending and in-progress requests, with room number and guest name.
3. Work with the relevant team (e.g., Housekeeping) to fulfill it, then update its status once complete.

### Checking Stock Levels
1. Go to **Inventory** to view current stock levels for hotel/restaurant supplies.

## 5. Status Meanings

**Bookings:** Confirmed (upcoming) · Checked In (currently staying) · Checked Out (stay complete) · Cancelled

**Service Requests:** Pending · In Progress · Completed

**Invoices:** Unpaid · Partially Paid · Paid

## 6. Common Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Check-In button isn't showing | The booking must be in Confirmed status — it won't show for bookings already Checked In, Checked Out, or Cancelled |
| Can't find a guest's booking | Use the search box, or ask the guest for their booking reference (starts with "BK") |
| Guest wants to check in but their booking dates don't start today | Bookings can be checked in on their scheduled date — if there's an issue, escalate to your Manager |

## 7. Important Notes

- Checking out a guest always generates their invoice automatically — you don't create invoices manually.
- Checking out also automatically creates a housekeeping task for that room.
- Cancellation may be subject to the hotel's cancellation policy — ask your Manager if you're unsure of current policy settings.

## 8. Your Permissions

As a Receptionist, you can manage bookings (check-in, check-out, cancel), view and help resolve service requests, view hotel invoices, and view inventory stock levels. You cannot access business reports, business rule settings, or restaurant-side screens.
