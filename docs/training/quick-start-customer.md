# Quick Start — Customer

## Your 4 Most Common Tasks

**1. Book a room:** Click **Hotel → Browse Hotels** → pick a hotel → pick dates → **Book Now**.

**2. Order food:** Click **Restaurant** (dine-in) or **Delivery** → pick a restaurant → add items → **Place Order**.

**3. Check everything at once:** Click **My Activity** (or your name → **My Account**) — one place for all your bookings, orders, reservations, bills, service requests, and notifications.

**4. Pay a bill:** **My Account → Invoices → Pay Now**.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- Can't find "new booking" in My Account? Booking always starts from the **Hotel** menu, not My Account.
- Need to cancel something? Go to the relevant tab in **My Account** and click **Cancel** — you'll always be asked to confirm first.
- Not sure about a policy (cancellation deadline, etc.)? Ask the chat assistant in the bottom-right corner.
