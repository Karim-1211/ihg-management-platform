# Quick Start — Receptionist

## Your 4 Most Common Tasks

**1. Check in a guest:** **Bookings** → find their Confirmed booking → **Check In** → confirm.

**2. Check out a guest:** **Bookings** → find their Checked In booking → **Check Out** → confirm. This auto-generates their invoice and creates a housekeeping task.

**3. Handle a service request:** **Service Requests** → work the request → mark **Completed** once done.

**4. Find a booking fast:** Use the search box at the top of **Bookings** — search by booking reference.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- No "Check In" button? The booking must be Confirmed — it won't show for other statuses.
- Guest wants to cancel? **Bookings** → find their booking → **Cancel** → confirm. Note: cancellation may be limited by policy close to check-in.
- Need to record supplies used/wasted? Go to **Inventory → Manage** on the item.
