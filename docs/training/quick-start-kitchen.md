# Quick Start — Kitchen

## Your 3 Most Common Tasks

**1. Prepare an order:** **Kitchen** → **Start Preparing** on a Confirmed order → mark **Ready** once done.

**2. Check ingredient stock:** **Inventory → Manage** on an item to see current stock.

**3. Record waste/spoilage:** **Inventory → Manage** → choose **Waste** → enter quantity → **Record**.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- Can't confirm/start an order? May be a stock shortage on a recipe-linked item — check **Inventory** or ask your Manager to restock.
- Recipe-linked stock deducts **automatically** when an order is confirmed — you don't need to record that usage manually.
